A note on reading the citations

The work nobody has done.

The frontier books cite real papers with real reference numbers, and anyone can pull them. Some support exactly what they are said to support. Some are accurate about a finding and then carry more interpretive weight than the paper itself claims. Some are correct in the citation and wrong in the summary.

Checking them one at a time is slow, and it is the only way to know which is which.

That work has not been done here. Doing it would be the most valuable next contribution anyone could make to this argument, from either direction.
